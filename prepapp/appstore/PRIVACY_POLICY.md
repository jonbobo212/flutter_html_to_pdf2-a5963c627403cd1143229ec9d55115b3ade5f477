## Who we are

Aspira ("we", "us") is a self-study and assessment toolkit that helps students
prepare for English exams (IELTS), learn languages, and explore universities and
scholarships. Aspira is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [ADDRESS/COUNTRY].
Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## What we collect

**You give us:**
- Profile: name, city, birth year, nationality, UI language, target language.
- Contact: email, phone number, Telegram username (all optional, used for
  sign-in, notifications, and application handoff).
- Goals: current/target English band, study level, education stage, dream field
  and target country, interests, target university.
- Your content: essays you write, placement-test and mock-test answers,
  and any text you submit to the AI adviser or essay checker.

**Created as you use the app:**
- Progress and activity: streaks, lessons/exercises completed, achievements.
- AI usage records: which AI features you used and token counts (for cost and
  quality monitoring). We log the *fact* of a call, not extra copies of your
  private text beyond what a feature needs to function.

**Automatically:**
- Referral/marketing attribution: referral code, `utm_source`, and how you
  arrived (e.g. a partner school or the T100U funnel).

We do **not** collect precise location, we do **not** use third-party
advertising, and we do **not** track you across other companies' apps or
websites.

## How we use it

- Provide the service: assessments, lessons, essay feedback, university and
  scholarship matching, streaks and achievements.
- Send notifications you opt into (e.g. Telegram study reminders). You can turn
  these off at any time (`notify_opt_out`).
- With your action, hand off your interest to a partner agency/university so they
  can help with an application.
- Improve quality and monitor AI cost. Aggregate, non-identifying analytics only.

## AI processing

Some features (adviser chat, essay feedback, translations) send your text to our
AI provider, **Anthropic (Claude)**, to generate a response. This text is
processed to produce your result and is **not used to train third-party models**.
See Anthropic's terms for their handling of API data.

## Who we share it with (processors)

- **Supabase** — database & hosting.
- **Anthropic** — AI processing of adviser/essay/translation text.
- **Vercel** — app hosting/delivery.
- **Telegram** — only if you enable Telegram notifications.
- **Partner agencies/universities** — only the details needed for an application
